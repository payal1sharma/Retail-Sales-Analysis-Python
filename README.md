# Retail-Sales-Analysis-Python
## Project Overview
This project presents an end-to-end Retail Sales Analysis using Python (Pandas), Matplotlib, and Microsoft Excel. The project focuses on cleaning and analyzing retail transaction data, visualizing key sales trends, and developing an interactive Excel dashboard to evaluate sales performance across regions, product categories, customers, and time, enabling data-driven business decisions.
## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Total Sales: $2,261,537
- Total Orders: 4,922
- Average Order Value: $459.48
- Product Categories: 3
- Sales Regions: 4
- Top Revenue Category: Technology ($827K)
- Highest Sales Region: West ($710K)
## Features
- KPI-based retail sales performance dashboard.
- Regional, category-wise, product, and customer analysis.
- Monthly sales trend and revenue distribution analysis.
- Interactive business reporting with Pivot Charts and visualizations.
## Insights & Conclusions
- Generated $2.26M in total sales across 4,922 customer orders, with an average order value of $459.48.
- Technology was the highest revenue-generating category ($827K), while the West region led sales with $710K.
- Sales peaked during November and December, indicating increased customer demand during the year-end season.
- Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier was the top-selling product, while Sean Miller emerged as the highest-value customer.
